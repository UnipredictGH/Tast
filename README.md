# UniPredict Ghana 🇬🇭

> **Helping Ghanaian students make confident, informed decisions about university admissions — before they apply.**

## About UniPredict

**UniPredict Ghana** is an **AI-powered education platform** built to help Ghanaian SHS students make confident, informed decisions about university admissions before they apply.

The platform brings university eligibility, admission requirements, scholarships, programme comparisons, and admissions guidance together in one accessible platform.

UniPredict allows students to:

- 🎓 Check which university programmes they qualify for based on their WASSCE results
- 🔎 Discover university programmes that match their qualifications
- 🎓 Discover scholarships they may be eligible for
- 📊 Compare programmes across Ghanaian universities
- 📚 Understand admission cut-off points and subject requirements
- 🤖 Get guidance through an AI-powered counsellor
- 📱 Access admissions information from a mobile phone

UniPredict currently covers **58 Ghanaian universities and more than 1,430 programmes**, with verified WASSCE cut-off and admission data.
